"Brown Bag" session recordings. This is the **official companion** to the synthesized study pack:
use the videos/decks to *learn the business*, and the `ai_help_docs/` + `SKILL_*.md` to go *code-deep*.

**Source folder:** CustomerExperience site → `Shared Documents / Training & Reference / Transportation / QPTM / QPTM 101`

## What QPTM 101 covers vs. what it doesn't

- **Covered (with video):** Pipeline Admin, Contracts, Rates, Nominations, Confirmations/Balancing, Allocations, Inventory, Billing — the core operational + foundation modules.
- **Not in this folder:** dedicated Capacity Release, Scheduling (CAS), Measurement, EDI, Penalties, Security decks. For those, use the synthesized `ai_help_docs/` + `SKILL_*.md`. (Related intro decks — e.g. "Pipeline 101" overview — live in the sibling `QPTM Overview` and other Quorum training sites.)
- **Numbering:** decks are `TRN_02`…`TRN_12`; 01/05/08/09 aren't in this folder (01 = the separate Pipeline 101 intro; others not published here).
